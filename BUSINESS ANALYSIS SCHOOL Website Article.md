## **4\. AI Business Strategy in 2027: Turning Artificial Intelligence into Business Value**

Artificial intelligence is creating new opportunities for businesses across different industries. Organisations are exploring how AI can improve productivity, enhance customer experiences, automate processes and support better decisions.

However, adopting AI does not automatically make a business more competitive.

The real value comes from understanding where AI can make a difference and developing a clear strategy for using it.

This is where AI business strategy becomes important.

**Why businesses need an AI strategy**

Without a clear strategy, organisations may invest in AI tools without understanding the problems they are expected to solve.

A business should first consider its objectives, current challenges and available opportunities before deciding which AI solutions to adopt.

For example, a company experiencing delays in customer support may consider using an AI-powered customer support system. However, before implementing the solution, it needs to understand the existing support process, identify the causes of delays and determine whether AI is an appropriate solution.

**Important areas businesses should consider**

**1\. Identifying business opportunities**

Organisations need to understand where AI can improve existing processes, solve problems or create new opportunities.

**2\. Prioritising AI initiatives**

Not every AI opportunity should be implemented immediately. Businesses need to evaluate potential benefits, costs, risks and feasibility.

**3\. Developing a business case**

An AI initiative should have a clear business justification. Organisations need to understand the expected value and resources required.

**4\. Responsible AI governance**

Data privacy, security, transparency and accountability should be considered when implementing AI solutions.

**5\. Managing implementation and change**

Introducing AI often affects existing processes and employees. Organisations need to plan implementation carefully and help people adapt.

**6\. Measuring results**

Businesses should establish clear measures to determine whether an AI initiative is achieving its intended objectives.

**What this means for business professionals**

Professionals who understand both business strategy and AI can help organisations move beyond experimenting with technology to creating meaningful business value.

The ability to identify opportunities, develop business cases, evaluate risks and support implementation will be increasingly relevant.

## **7\. AI Literacy vs. Technical Expertise: What Do Business Professionals Need in 2027?**

Artificial intelligence is becoming part of everyday business activities. Professionals across different industries are being encouraged to understand how AI works and how it can support their responsibilities.

But does every business professional need to become a programmer or technical expert to remain relevant?

Not necessarily.

There is a difference between AI literacy and technical expertise.

**What is AI literacy?**

AI literacy means understanding what AI tools can do, how to use them appropriately and when their outputs should be questioned or verified.

For example, a business professional may use AI to summarise information, prepare reports, organise tasks or support research.

**What is technical expertise?**

Technical expertise involves deeper knowledge of technology, which may include programming, software development, machine learning or building AI systems.

These skills are important in specialised technical roles, but not every business professional needs to develop them to use AI effectively.

**Why AI literacy matters**

Business professionals need to understand how AI can support their work without depending on it blindly.

Some important abilities include:

* Writing clear and effective prompts.  
* Selecting appropriate AI tools.  
* Reviewing AI-generated information.  
* Protecting confidential business information.  
* Identifying tasks that can be automated.  
* Understanding the limitations of AI.

**AI skills must be connected to business knowledge**

Knowing how to use an AI tool is useful, but understanding the business problem is equally important.

For example, using AI to generate a business strategy does not automatically mean the strategy is appropriate. Someone still needs to understand the organisation, its objectives, its customers and its operating environment.

The most effective professionals will be able to combine business understanding with practical AI skills.

## **9\. Beyond ChatGPT: How AI Agents Are Changing the Way Businesses Work**

Many professionals are familiar with AI chat tools that answer questions, generate content and summarise information.

However, artificial intelligence is developing beyond simple conversations. One area receiving increasing attention is AI agents.

An AI agent is designed to work towards a defined goal by carrying out a sequence of tasks. Depending on its configuration, it may use connected tools, retrieve information and perform actions with varying levels of human supervision.

**How are AI agents different from basic AI chat tools?**

A basic AI chat tool may help you draft a customer response.

An AI agent, on the other hand, may be configured to review a customer request, retrieve relevant information, prepare a response and send it for approval.

The difference is that an AI agent can be designed to carry out connected tasks rather than simply respond to one instruction.

**How businesses can use AI agents**

**1\. Customer support**

AI agents can help respond to common enquiries, organise support requests and direct customers to relevant information.

**2\. Lead generation**

Businesses can use AI-supported systems to organise prospect information and support lead research and follow-up processes.

**3\. Administrative automation**

AI can support repetitive activities such as organising files, preparing summaries and managing routine tasks.

**4\. Research and analysis**

AI agents can assist with collecting information, organising findings and preparing initial reports for review.

**5\. Business operations**

Organisations can explore AI agents to support workflows across different departments.

**What should professionals consider?**

Although AI agents offer opportunities, businesses must pay attention to data privacy, accuracy, access permissions and human supervision.

Not every task should be automated, and AI-generated outputs should be reviewed before important decisions are made.

**What this means for professionals**

Understanding AI agents can help professionals identify opportunities to improve productivity and business operations.

You do not need to build an AI system from scratch to benefit from understanding how these tools work. You need to know where they can be useful and how to apply them responsibly.

## **10\. From AI Prompts to AI Workflows: How Professionals Can Work Smarter**

Many people begin their AI journey by asking questions or giving instructions to tools such as ChatGPT.

This is known as prompting.

A prompt is an instruction given to an AI tool to generate a response or complete a task. For example, you may ask an AI tool to summarise a meeting, draft an email or explain a business concept.

However, AI can support more than individual tasks.

Professionals can also create AI-supported workflows that connect different activities into a repeatable process.

**What is an AI workflow?**

An AI workflow is a sequence of connected tasks in which AI supports one or more stages of a process.

For example, imagine a business receives customer enquiries through an online form.

An AI-supported workflow could:

1. Collect incoming enquiries.  
2. Organise the information.  
3. Categorise the requests.  
4. Prepare initial responses.  
5. Send complex requests to a human support representative.

Instead of manually handling every step, the business can use automation to support parts of the process.

**Why should professionals understand AI workflows?**

AI workflows can help reduce repetitive work, improve consistency and free up time for activities that require human judgement.

They can be useful in areas such as:

* Customer support.  
* Administrative operations.  
* Lead management.  
* Research and reporting.  
* File and task management.  
* Business process improvement.

**What skills do you need?**

To work effectively with AI workflows, professionals should understand how to:

* Give clear instructions to AI tools.  
* Identify repetitive tasks.  
* Understand how business processes operate.  
* Select appropriate tools.  
* Review AI-generated outputs.  
* Recognise privacy and security risks.

The goal is not to automate everything. It is to identify where AI can make work more efficient without compromising quality.

## **11\. Agentic AI Explained: What Business Professionals Should Know in 2027**

Artificial intelligence is moving beyond tools that simply generate responses. A growing area of interest is agentic AI.

Agentic AI refers to systems designed to pursue specific goals by planning and carrying out a series of actions. Depending on how they are built, these systems may use tools, interact with other applications and adjust their approach based on the results they receive.

For business professionals, understanding this development is becoming increasingly relevant.

**How does agentic AI work?**

Imagine a business wants to improve its customer support process.

A conventional AI chatbot may answer customer questions using information it has been provided.

An AI agent may be configured to perform several connected activities, such as reviewing a request, retrieving relevant information, preparing a response and escalating an issue when necessary.

The level of independence depends on how the system is designed and the permissions it receives.

**Where can businesses apply agentic AI?**

Some possible applications include:

* Customer support and service operations.  
* Business research and information gathering.  
* Lead management and follow-up.  
* Administrative task automation.  
* Document processing.  
* Internal workflow coordination.

**What are the risks?**

As AI systems become capable of performing more tasks, organisations must pay attention to how they are used.

Important considerations include:

* Protecting sensitive information.  
* Ensuring the accuracy of outputs.  
* Limiting access to important systems.  
* Maintaining human oversight.  
* Defining accountability for actions.  
* Monitoring performance and potential errors.

**Why should business professionals pay attention?**

Agentic AI may change how organisations manage workflows and allocate responsibilities.

Business professionals who understand processes, identify opportunities for improvement and evaluate technology-based solutions can contribute to these changes.

The important thing is to understand the business need before deciding how AI should be applied.

## **12\. How to Identify Business Problems Before Recommending AI Solutions**

Artificial intelligence is attracting attention across different industries. Businesses are exploring ways to use AI to improve operations, reduce repetitive work and support decision-making.

However, one mistake organisations can make is choosing a technology before understanding the problem they are trying to solve.

Not every business challenge requires an AI solution.

Sometimes, the real issue may be an unclear process, poor communication, inadequate training or a lack of defined responsibilities.

This is why identifying the actual business problem should come before recommending a solution.

**Step 1: Understand the business objective**

Start by asking what the organisation wants to achieve.

Is it trying to improve customer satisfaction, reduce operational delays, increase productivity or improve the quality of its services?

**Step 2: Understand the current situation**

Examine how the existing process works. Speak with relevant stakeholders and gather information about the challenges they experience.

**Step 3: Identify the root cause**

Do not assume that the first problem reported is the actual cause.

Investigate what may be contributing to the situation and determine why the problem exists.

**Step 4: Explore possible solutions**

Consider different options. These may include improving an existing process, changing responsibilities, providing training or introducing technology.

**Step 5: Evaluate whether AI is appropriate**

If AI could help, consider the expected benefits, costs, risks, data requirements and implementation challenges.

**Step 6: Develop a clear business case**

Explain why the proposed solution is necessary, what it is expected to achieve and how its success will be measured.

**Step 7: Measure the outcome**

After implementing a solution, evaluate whether it has achieved the intended objective. Use relevant performance measures to understand what has improved and what still needs attention.

**Why this matters**

AI can support business improvement, but it cannot replace the need to understand business needs.

Professionals who can identify problems, evaluate options and connect solutions to organisational goals will be valuable in an environment where technology continues to change.
