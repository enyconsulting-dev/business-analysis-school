About Us

Built by a Career Changer, for Career Changers

Business Analysis School was founded by Eno Eka — an accountant who transitioned into business analysis, grew her income to six figures, and became one of the most recognized thought leaders in the global BA community.

After relocating to Canada in 2018 and landing a six-figure consulting role within six months, Eno started coaching other professionals on how to make the same transition. What began as informal mentoring grew into a full education platform that has now trained over 100,000 professionals across 90+ countries.
